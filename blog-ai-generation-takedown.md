## What Happened

As of today, **October 28, 2026**, wallpepersphere has permanently retired its AI image generation feature. This includes the generator page, all AI generation tools, and any associated endpoints.

All links to these features have been formally decommissioned and the routes are no longer reachable on the platform.

This was a decision made by **Asanji Bruno**, founder and sole operator of wallpepersphere.com.

---

## Why We Made This Decision

  <div style="border-left:4px solid #2980b9;padding:12px 16px;border-radius:6px;flex:1;min-width:200px;">
    <strong>🎯 Sharper Focus</strong><br>Wallpepersphere's core strength has always been its curated image gallery — not generation. We're doubling down on what we do best.

  <div style="border-left:4px solid #27ae60;padding:12px 16px;border-radius:6px;flex:1;min-width:200px;">
    <strong>👥 Real Community Content</strong><br>We are moving toward a platform built on real images uploaded by real people — authentic, human-curated content.

  <div style="border-left:4px solid #8e44ad;padding:12px 16px;border-radius:6px;flex:1;min-width:200px;">

The AI generation feature served its purpose as an early experiment. It helped us understand what our users actually want — and what they want is **quality, curated, real wallpapers.** Not generated filler.

The internet already has enough AI-generated content. Wallpepersphere is choosing a different path.

---

## What This Means in Practice

**🚫 No longer available:**
- `/generator` — AI wallpaper generation page
- `/ai` — AI image tools
- Any in-app AI generation controls previously visible on image pages

**✅ Everything else remains fully operational:**
- The full 4K wallpaper gallery — **[wallpepersphere.com](https://wallpepersphere.com/)**
- All categories — **[wallpepersphere.com/category](https://wallpepersphere.com/category)**
- Free downloads — no account required
- Image uploads by the community
- All aesthetic collections (Dreamcore, Voidcore, Frutiger Aero, and more)

## What Comes Next

Wallpepersphere is now **fully focused on building the best curated 4K wallpaper gallery on the web** — powered entirely by real uploaded images from real people.
